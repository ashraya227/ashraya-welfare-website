Mental Health Awareness in Telangana

Mental health is an important part of overall health. It affects how people think, feel, manage stress, build relationships and make decisions. Ashraya Welfare and Educational Society aims to promote mental health awareness, reduce stigma and encourage supportive communities.

```

Why Mental Health Awareness Matters

People may experience stress, anxiety, sadness, family difficulties and other emotional challenges at different stages of life. Learning about mental health helps individuals recognise when they need support and encourages families and communities to respond with understanding.

  • Promoting emotional well-being and healthy coping skills.
  • Reducing stigma and misconceptions about mental health.
  • Encouraging people to seek professional help when needed.
  • Supporting families, students, workers and young people.

Youth Mental Health

Young people may face academic pressure, unemployment concerns, relationship difficulties, social pressure and uncertainty about the future. A caring environment, healthy routines and access to trusted support can help them manage these challenges.

Parents, teachers and community members can help by listening without judgement, respecting young people's feelings and guiding them towards qualified professionals when necessary.

Stress Management and Emotional Well-being

  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Make time for physical activity and relaxation.
  • Stay connected with trusted family members and friends.
  • Break difficult tasks into manageable steps.
  • Seek professional support if emotional difficulties persist or interfere with daily life.

These steps may support general well-being, but they do not replace assessment or treatment by a qualified mental health professional.

Building Supportive Families and Communities

Families, schools, workplaces and community organisations can contribute to mental health awareness by encouraging respectful conversations, identifying support needs early and helping people access appropriate services.

Ashraya Welfare and Educational Society supports awareness and community education initiatives intended to encourage understanding, inclusion and emotional well-being.

Get Support

If you or someone you know is experiencing emotional distress, consider speaking with a qualified mental health professional or contacting an appropriate health service.

In India, Tele-MANAS provides mental health support at 14416. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.
```
