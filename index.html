Who we are

Care that begins with listening.

Ashraya Welfare and Educational Society is a community-focused organisation committed to welfare, education and public awareness. We believe every person deserves respect, reliable information and the opportunity to live a safe, healthy and dignified life.

Through awareness activities, community engagement and collaboration, we aim to encourage early help-seeking, reduce stigma and connect people with appropriate support services.

What we care about

Our areas of focus

Practical awareness and community action can help people make informed choices and find support.

🧠

Mental Health Awareness

Promote understanding of stress, emotional wellbeing and mental health concerns. Encourage respectful conversations and timely help-seeking.

🌱

Drug Prevention & Recovery Awareness

Share prevention information, challenge stigma and encourage people affected by substance use to seek qualified professional help.

🧒

Child Welfare

Support awareness of child safety, children’s rights, positive parenting and the importance of listening to and protecting children.

⛏️

Workers & Families

Promote wellbeing awareness and community support for workers and their families, including communities connected with Singareni.

📚

Education & Awareness

Encourage learning, life skills, health awareness and access to useful information for children, youth and adults.

🤝

Community Welfare

Bring volunteers and community partners together to identify local needs and support inclusive, compassionate initiatives.
