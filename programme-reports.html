Our Programme Reports

Promoting mental health awareness, substance-use prevention, family well-being, and healthier communities.

1. Mental Health Awareness Programmes

Mental health awareness helps people understand emotional well-being, reduce stigma, recognize when support may be needed, and seek appropriate professional help.

Focus areas: Emotional well-being, stress management, awareness, and early support.

2. Drug and Substance Abuse Prevention

These awareness initiatives aim to educate students, young people, workers, and families about the risks associated with substance use and the importance of healthy choices.

Focus areas: Prevention education, peer pressure, healthy coping skills, family support, and access to professional help.

3. Singareni Workers and Family Awareness

Community awareness can encourage conversations about stress, mental well-being, family communication, substance-use prevention, and available support services.

Focus areas: Worker and family well-being, awareness, prevention, and supportive community relationships.

Programme details: Add confirmed programme dates, locations, activities, and results after the relevant activities take place.

4. Youth and Student Awareness

Educational awareness activities can help young people develop decision-making skills, respond to peer pressure, build healthy relationships, and choose positive ways to manage stress.

Focus areas: Life skills, healthy habits, self-confidence, and substance-use prevention.
